      WHAT THIS AGENT IS
      One useful step in the venue sales journey.
      This is an inbound lead-response assistant, sometimes called a wedding enquiry drafting agent. It supports the first response to a prospective couple, so it is GTM-adjacent.
      It is not a broad GTM agent: it does not find prospects, run campaigns, manage a pipeline, check dates or send messages. A person reviews every draft.
      THE PROMISEA faster, more consistent first draft, with uncertainty visible before it reaches a couple.

      01
      Rooms have different limits
      Orangery: 144 ceremony, 140 wedding breakfast, 280 reception. Garden Room: 100 ceremony and reception. Burdett Suite: 200 ceremony, 240 wedding breakfast, 300 reception.
      
        
      02
      Prices need a team check
      The 2026 brochure lists £165 + VAT per person for 100–150 guests and £155 + VAT above 150. The Garden Room page says packages from £155 + VAT, minimum 100. The pages differ.
      
        
      03
      No live diary connection
      Date availability and viewing times must be checked with No.11’s team. The demo does not invent a slot or imply a date is free.
      
        
      04
      People handle exceptions
      Dietary specifics, accessibility needs, policy questions and contract terms are surfaced for a person to confirm.
